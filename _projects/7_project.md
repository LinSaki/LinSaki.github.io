Developed a lighthearted third-person shooter game as the final project for a 3D game programming course. Implemented ray casting for shooting mechanics, allowing players to eliminate zombies and earn points for each successful hit. Designed gameplay around checkpoint collection to reward players with additional time and bonus points. Integrated custom character and city models, event-triggered animations, and background music inspired by favorite video games to enhance the experience.
