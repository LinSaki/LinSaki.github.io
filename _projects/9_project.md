A 2D RPG platformer currently in development using Unity. The mini-game centers around a fast-paced combat scenario where the player must defend a friend from waves of enemies using a sword. The game features responsive controls and action-driven mechanics designed for short, engaging gameplay sessions. Future plans include expanding character abilities, adding environmental hazards, and refining visual effects to enhance player immersion.
